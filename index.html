NSE trading calendar

Holidays are pulled from the exchange by the Sheet backend on a daily timer and stored there. NSE publishes one calendar year at a time, so future years fill in as the exchange releases them; until then those weeks are marked provisional.

Add or replace a year by hand

One holiday per line as YYYY-MM-DD, Occasion. Use this to load a year NSE has circulated but the API has not yet served. A manual year is protected — the auto-fetch will not overwrite it with a thinner list.
